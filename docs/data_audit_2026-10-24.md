## 3. Method

- **Steps.** A sheet row is a step unless it is a `dupli` row (a second photograph of the state before it); the collapsed steps are numbered on without them (spec 2.2, v1.25). Folder counts are the step folders on the drive (Camera_1/Camera_2/RealSense `NNN`, scanner `RGB<n>1`).
- **Change magnitudes.** Every frame is cut to the chassis ROI (the database's pose-segment ROI, else the box `tda.core.cache.suggest_roi_over` proposes, else the whole frame), turned grey, downscaled to at most 256 px and normalised by its median and MAD. The change between two frames is their mean absolute difference.
- **Hypotheses.** The scanner folders are mapped onto the OAK rows as the index declares (`as declared`), and by one or two edits of that: the scanner skipped row k, folder n re-shoots the previous state, or both (a skip compensated by an extra shot -- no count symptom). The score of a mapping is the Spearman rank correlation of the scanner's folder-to-folder changes with the OAK changes between the rows it pairs. Mappings that show the same states (a `dupli` row shows the row before it) are one hypothesis.
- **Verdict.** Thousands of mappings are tried, so one always edges the declared mapping out by noise: on the desktops whose declaration was checked by eye the best single edit still scored up to +0.09 and the best skip + re-shot pair up to +0.21. So every edit costs 0.11 of score, and a mapping replaces the declared one only when it beats it by more than its edits cost, by at least 0.05, and in at least 95 % of 200 paired bootstrap resamples of the folder pairs. `aligned` otherwise (**FLAG** when such a mapping exists but wins too few resamples); `skip` / `dup` / `skip+dup` = the kind of the mapping that won (`skip rows a-b`: the positions as plausible as the best, i.e. not beaten in 90 % of the resamples); `unknown` = even the best correlates below 0.30. The cell's `(vs ...)` is the best rival and its score relative to the declared mapping.
- **Scanner pictures vs the sheet** (`scripts/step_changes.py`, section 2c). For every step of the collapsed sheet, the scanner frame of step s-1 and of step s (on the steps the U25 index gives them; each burst stands for the shot the local cache shows, so an unexposed, blown-out or outlier `P_0` is replaced -- section 2d) are cut to the chassis ROI, downscaled to 256 px, the later one matched to the earlier one's 10th and 90th percentiles, and a pixel counts as changed when the 5x5-blurred difference exceeds 25 grey levels (3x3 opening removes specks). No registration: the scanner is fixed. Over the 2,732 steps the changed fraction has medians of 0.4 % (unscrew), 1.3 % (open), 2.2 % (disconnect), 3.8 % (remove), 20 % (displace) and 86 % (reorient), and 95th percentiles of 5-10 % for the quiet verbs, which sets the thresholds: (a) remove / displace of a part visible from above with < 0.5 %; (b) only unscrew / release (> 15 %), disconnect (> 25 %) or a latch opened (> 20 %); (c) first vs last < 15 %. RAM modules, CPUs and expansion cards are not held to (a): edge-on or a few pixels square at 256 px, their visible removals measure under 0.5 %. A step that hits (a) is not tested for (b). The sheet's step of a scanner step is the same number where the sheet's rows are the Camera_1 captures; where they are not, it is found by the rows' Sequence Numbers (capture k = the rows numbered k; D56's skipped 41 leaves a capture without a row, D63's repeated 11-13 put two rows on one capture), and a desktop whose numbers cannot say it (D43: 47 rows numbered 1-47 for 48 captures) is not checked. The measure compares grey levels, so a lamp or glare that lights one part of the chassis and not the rest is a change too: the **by eye** notes in 2c say which flags are that.
- **RealSense column.** The same check against the RealSense frames where every row has one -- a second opinion only: its view of the chassis is smaller and the correlations are lower (a third of the desktops below 0.3), so a RealSense flag alone is not acted on.
- **What the numbers cannot do.** A run of small changes (screws, connectors) gives near-equal scores to every skip position inside it, so a skip is localised to an interval, not a row; on D48 and D62 the best position was wrong while the sheet's own words ("Dupli (scanner does not scan)") and the frames agreed. Every declaration in `configs/index_fixes.yaml` was therefore settled by looking: the *scanner changes* crops show the two places consecutive scanner folders differ most -- a part gone, or only the light and the background (a re-shot).
- **On the threshold.** These picture flags are within 10 % of their limit, so a slightly different ROI or shot would flip them: D02 step 33 26.9% (limit 25%), D37 step 45 0.5% (limit 0.5%), D45 step 11 25.2% (limit 25%).
